⛏️ Funshotbuild

Build with blocks. Survive 70 armed bots.

  • 📱 Touch: left stick = move, drag right side = look, Fire / Jump / Break / Place buttons
  • 🖥️ Desktop: WASD, Space, click = shoot, right-click = place, E = break, 1-7 = blocks
  • ⌨️ Mouse + keyboard on a tablet/phone: they work alongside the touch controls. Tap 🖐 on the side (or press T) to hide the touch controls
  • 💣 TNT: button or Q. Explodes after 3s: 50 damage, knockback, 2s ragdoll
  • 👁 V = first/third person · 😀 G = emotes · K = skins
